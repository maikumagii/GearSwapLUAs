Why COR?
- Very cheap to start providing direct buffs to party. Buy a (Barataria Ring)[https://www.ffxiah.com/item/28548/barataria-ring], and you're Rolls are going to be about 80% as strong as someone who maxed out a (Rostam)[https://www.ffxiah.com/item/21581/rostam]. Don't get me wrong - you'll want a Rostam eventually but the barrier to entry is super cheap to be effective as a party buffer with no gear.
- Gearing up the DD for COR is also cheap, and overlaps with many other jobs. Savage Blade spam via Naegling is COR's main way to DD, if you're entertaining leveling other jobs that can use Naegling (about half of the other jobs), gearing COR for a TP/WS set towards Naegling is likely to be Best in Slot (BiS) for them as well.
- Can reset job abilities, upgrade debuffs on monsters, dispel/sleep -- aka COR does it all.

How strong is COR (and their buffs)?
- Extremely strong, but because ATK buff is % based, it pairs well with other buffers but is weaker if the party you're buffing isn't geared. 

Why not COR? 
- No group haste option. Does get a delay reduction roll that works similar to Dual Wield but is multiplicative as opposed to additive.
- Oversaturated. Because of everything above (cheap and high demand) doesn't necessarily offset the fact that the job is also in high supply.
- Can get expensive the further down the COR rabbit hole you go: the more you want to be an "excellent" COR the more you realize you're essentially gearing four different jobs in one.

Why GEO?
- GEO Buffs are incredibly busted, especially their debuff bubbles.
- High demand, low supply - will find a role easily in end game content.
- Geomancy from Dunna is easy to get through Oboro, but still only half of additional effectiveness of an Idris.

How strong is GEO (and their buffs)?
- Buffs are some of the strongest and their nuking is great as Magic Bursts. 
- A maxed Geomancy Fury/Frailty without using cooldowns is essentially a 2.78 multiplier to damage (Physical Damage Limit permitting). No other class has a two hit combo like that that multiplies all damage by nearly a factor of 3.

Why not GEO? 
- Reliant on Geomancy, and Geomancy Skill + Handbell Skill to reach maximum potential. 
- Geomancy Skill + Handbell Skill should equal 900 combined to maximize the potency of your spells. This requires heavy gearing and mastering your job.
- Geomancy from Dunna is easy to get, but Idris is timegated to multiple months and completing a bunch of tedious tasks and all Adoulin quests.
- Because GEO is _so_ strong, it's nerfed in many end game content, somtimes only offering 25% of their original benefit (and are still strong). This nerf was intended to nerf Idris GEOs, but makes you feel extra weak starting out.

Why BRD?
- BRD can make undergeared teammates feel like gods because the benefits are flat scaling off your gear rather than theirs.
- March haste caps and is part of your rotation rather than COR and GEO who have to sacrifice one of their buffs to provide additional haste.
- BRD also grants accuracy as part of their buffs, which is needed at the highest levels of gameplay.

How strong is BRD (and their buffs)?
- Incredibly strong, Marches and Minuets carry teams.

Why not BRD? 
- Most REMA intensive job in the game, your power scales with each REMA you gain.
- Elitist groups will filter based on how many REMAs you have. 
- DD BRD is viable, but generally TP gear doesn't support it very well.
